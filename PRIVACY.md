# Privacy Policy

AIsa Privacy Policy

Last update: March 10, 2026

By accessing or using the AIsa Services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy. Capitalized terms not defined in this Privacy Policy have the meanings given to them in the AIsa Services Agreement.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

### 1.1 Information You Provide to Us

When you create an AIsa Account, use the AIsa Services, or communicate with us, you may provide us with the following types of information:
- Account Information. When you register for an AIsa Account, we collect your name, email address, and any other information you provide during the registration process.
- Payment and Billing Information. When you purchase Credits or conduct Transactions through the AIsa Services, we collect payment-related information necessary to process your transactions. This may include billing address and payment method details. We use third-party payment processors and do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
- Communications. When you contact us for support, provide feedback, or otherwise communicate with us, we collect the content of those communications, including any attachments or files you submit.
- User Information. Information you provide to initiate and maintain your AIsa Account, which may include information about your business entity, representatives, beneficial owners, and other individuals associated with your Account.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access or use the AIsa Services, we automatically collect certain information, including:
- Usage Data. Information about how you interact with the AIsa Services, including the features you use, the pages you visit, the actions you take, the time and duration of your activities, and other usage statistics.
- API Usage Data. When you use the AIsa API, we collect metadata about your API requests, including request timestamps, endpoints accessed, model identifiers, token counts, response times, and error codes. This data is used for billing, performance monitoring, abuse prevention, and service improvement.
- Device and Connection Information. Information about the device and internet connection you use to access the AIsa Services, including your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, and network information.
- Cookies and Similar Technologies. We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activity on the Site. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

### 1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third parties, including Connected Service Providers, identity verification services, fraud prevention services, and publicly available sources. We may combine this information with other information we collect about you.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
- Providing and Maintaining the AIsa Services. To operate, maintain, and improve the AIsa Services, including processing Transactions, managing your AIsa Account, routing API requests to Connected Service Providers, and providing customer support.
- Billing and Payments. To process Credit purchases, calculate usage-based charges, manage your AIsa Wallet balance, and facilitate payments to and from Connected Service Providers.
- Security and Fraud Prevention. To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent, unauthorized, or illegal activity, and to protect the rights, property, and safety of AIsa, our users, and others.
- Compliance. To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, and governmental requests, including anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-customer (KYC) requirements.
- Communications. To send you service-related notices, updates, security alerts, and administrative messages. With your consent, we may also send you promotional communications, which you can opt out of at any time.
- Analytics and Improvement. To analyze usage patterns, monitor the performance and reliability of the AIsa Services, and develop new features and services.
- Personalization. To personalize your experience with the AIsa Services, including by providing tailored content and recommendations.

## 3. AI-Specific Data Practices

### 3.1 API Request and Response Data

When you use the AIsa API to access AI models and Connected Services, your API requests (including prompts) and the corresponding responses are transmitted through our infrastructure to the applicable Connected Service Provider. AIsa processes this data to route requests, calculate billing, and ensure service quality.

### 3.2 Data Retention of API Content

AIsa does not retain the content of your API requests or responses (such as prompts and completions) beyond the time necessary to process and deliver them, unless required for billing disputes, fraud investigation, or compliance with legal obligations. Metadata associated with API requests (such as timestamps, token counts, and model identifiers) may be retained for billing and analytics purposes.

### 3.3 Connected Service Provider Data Practices

When your API requests are routed to a Connected Service Provider, that provider may have its own data retention and privacy practices. AIsa is not responsible for the data practices of Connected Service Providers. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any Connected Service Provider whose services you access through AIsa.

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information in the following circumstances:
- Connected Service Providers. We share information necessary to route your API requests and facilitate Transactions with the Connected Service Providers you choose to access through the AIsa Services. This may include authentication credentials and Access Authorizations.
- Service Providers. We share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as payment processing, data analytics, email delivery, hosting, customer support, and identity verification. These service providers are contractually obligated to use your information only as necessary to provide services to us.
- Legal Requirements. We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of AIsa, our users, or others.
- Business Transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, sale of assets, or bankruptcy, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity or successor.
- With Your Consent. We may share your information with third parties when you have given us your explicit consent to do so.
- Aggregated or De-identified Data. We may share aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you for research, analytics, and other purposes.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your AIsa Account is active or as needed to provide you with the AIsa Services. We may also retain your information as necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and for legitimate business purposes such as maintaining security and preventing fraud.

When your AIsa Account is closed or terminated, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required or permitted by applicable law. API usage metadata may be retained in aggregated or anonymized form for analytics and service improvement purposes.

## 6. Data Security

We implement commercially reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational measures designed to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encryption of data in transit and at rest, access controls, regular security assessments, and employee training.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your AIsa Account credentials and for any activity that occurs under your Account.
